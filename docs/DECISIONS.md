# Decisions

Binding for everyone working on the code, including coding agents ([AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) §5). One row per decision; open questions
are added as `open` with options and a recommendation, and nobody decides them in code. IDs continue the hackathon repo's decision log.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| D-07 | 13 Nov 2026 | Pill Snap (iPhone app + web app) is the product, renamed **BlisterKompass** (D-14). No port into a Python backend. | decided | It is what the team built and demoed; one product, two clients ([AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) §3). |
| D-09 | 13 Nov 2026 | May a patient print carry the physician's BMP 2.8 code? | open | Until decided: no code on patient prints; the sheet is titled "Meine Medikamente mit Bildern" and the app never builds a BMP code from the phone's list. Options: (a) no code (current); (b) keep the original scanned bytes encrypted on the device with consent and reprint them only while the list is unchanged, which means storing `P` (name, birth date, insurance number). There is no physician end anymore (D-12), so the former option "the physician end prints it" is gone. Recommendation: (a). Blocks only putting a code back on the print. |
| D-10 | 13 Nov 2026 | May the plan reader keep the print date `A/@t` to show "Gehört zum Medikationsplan vom …"? | open | Until decided: the `A` element stays unread, as the privacy rule says. `A/@t` is a timestamp, not person data, but it sits in the issuer element (practice name, LANR, address) the app promises never to read. Option: read only `A/@t` and document it as the one exception. Recommendation: allow it; it links the sheet and the phone's list to a plan version. |
| D-11 | 13 Nov 2026 | May a print show "Ihre Wochenbox laut Medikationsplan" (7 days × 4 slots, the plan's pills per compartment, split ½/¼ tablets)? | open | Proposed on a branch in the hackathon repo, not ported. INV-16/17: a picture of what belongs in each compartment is close to filling a dosette by picture, and changes to prepared doses belong to the responsible professional. Options: (a) leave it out; (b) a separate page labelled "nach Plan, Box füllt Apotheke/Pflegedienst" with no filling instructions, after a pharmacist check. Recommendation: (b), after a pharmacist review. The mascot picture `docs/brand/mascot-week.png` (a pill put into a weekly box) is not used for the same reason. |
| D-12 | 13 Nov 2026 | **Scope: no physician app.** Two ends: the older person (the printable sheet, [PRD](PRD-print-sheet.md)) and the carer or skilled patient (the scanner app, [PRD](PRD-scanner-app.md)). | decided (team lead) | Focus for the pitch and after; a separate clinician product is out of scope. The paper BMP from the practice stays the professional's artefact. |
