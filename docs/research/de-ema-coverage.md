# How much of a German polypharmacy plan has free (EMA) appearance data?

Data year 2025, researched 13.11.2026. **Source note:** the Arzneiverordnungs-Report itself (Springer) was not accessible. All numbers come from the same underlying data, the WIdO GKV-Arzneimittelindex: the free tool PharMaAnalyst (data year 2025, updated 08.10.2026) and WIdO e-Papers 17/18 (Oct/Nov 2026). EMA status per substance comes from EMA's spreadsheet of centrally authorised products (2,760 rows, downloaded 13.11.2026).

## Key numbers

| | |
|---|---|
| GKV 2025 | 750.8 Mio prescriptions, 50.33 bn DDD, €62.6 bn net cost; patent-protected drugs = 7.2 % of DDD but 55 % of cost |
| Generics | 80.6 % of GKV DDD (Pro Generika/IGES); 73.5 % of generic packs dispensed under a Rabattvertrag |
| Top 50 substances (55.7 % of all prescriptions) | only **5–7.5 %** of their prescriptions are centrally authorised |
| **Typical older multi-medication regimen** (cardiovascular, antithrombotics, diabetes, thyroid, PPIs, gout; 88 substances) | **11.8–15.8 % of prescriptions, 9.3–12.6 % of DDD central** |
| Whole GKV market | about 6–10 % central (lower bound 5.75 % of prescriptions, 6.8 % of DDD) |
| Older patients (WIdO, data 2022) | **43 %** of GKV insured aged 65+ took **more than five** substances at the same time; 50.3 % got at least one PRISCUS-2.0 drug; people 65+ account for 56 % of all GKV DDD |
| Manufacturer switches (WIdO, AOK long-term patients, 2016) | **15 %** changed manufacturer within the year; on average 27 products per generic substance (2017), > 500 for some |

**Pitch line:** about 9 in 10 tablets an older polypharmacy patient takes are national generics with no free EMA appearance description, and those are exactly the products that switch manufacturer under rebate contracts.

## Coverage by scope

| Scope | Prescriptions central | DDD central |
|---|---|---|
| Top 30 substances by prescriptions | 4.6–6.4 % | 4.6–5.2 % |
| Top 50 substances by prescriptions | 5.1–7.5 % | 5.1–6.5 % |
| Older-patient basket (88 substances) | 11.8–15.8 % | 9.3–12.6 % |
| Whole GKV | ≥ 5.75 % (up to ~9.1 %) | ≥ 6.8 % (up to ~10.2 %) |

Method: substance totals for 2025 from 61 ATC level-4 groups (160 substances, 70.3 % of prescriptions, 80.2 % of DDD), each classified against the EMA list. Lower bound counts only central volumes found by name; upper bound counts a mixed substance as fully central.

## Caveats

- Only 61 ATC groups were queried; a few high-DDD items outside them are missing.
- Central ≠ appearance in hand: EMA product information describes central originals; national generics of mixed substances are not covered.
- EMA list membership was not checked against German marketing status.
- Not verified against a primary source: BARMER polypharmacy shares, the Kesselheim 2014 odds ratios (pill colour change +34 %, shape change +66 % odds of stopping), the 16,962 rebate contracts.
- Older-patient figures are 2022 data; no newer WIdO update found.
